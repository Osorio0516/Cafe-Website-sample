Description

Steam & Sip is a cozy, modern coffee shop offering premium brews and handcrafted beverages in a warm, inviting atmosphere. Perfect for coffee lovers, students, and professionals, it combines quality drinks with a relaxing space for work, study, or casual meetups.

Mission

To create a welcoming space where people can connect, relax, and recharge while enjoying high-quality, ethically sourced coffee and handcrafted beverages. We aim to blend exceptional taste with a cozy atmosphere that inspires creativity and community.

Menu Section:

Caramel Latte

A smooth blend of freshly brewed espresso, steamed milk, and rich caramel syrup, topped with a light foam and a drizzle of caramel. Perfect for those who love a sweet, comforting pick‑me‑up.

Matcha Green Tea Latte

Earthy Japanese matcha whisked into creamy steamed milk, lightly sweetened, and finished with a delicate foam. A refreshing choice for tea lovers seeking a calm energy boost.

Blueberry Muffin

Freshly baked, fluffy muffin bursting with juicy blueberries and a hint of vanilla. Ideal for pairing with your morning coffee or as a sweet afternoon treat.
